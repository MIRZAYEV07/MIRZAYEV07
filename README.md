Senior Backend Engineer with 6+ years of high-load Python for government and enterprise.

**Census.** I took part in Uzbekistan's national population and agricultural census from beginning to end: more than 40 million registered. I contributed technical solutions throughout, including these:

- On **minisurvey.stat.uz**, the pre-census platform for registering and mapping households, I built the auth service (2.5K+ RPS) and tuned PostgreSQL writes.
- On **census.stat.uz**, the census platform itself, I worked on real-time statistics over 5+ TB of data in PostgreSQL and ClickHouse, at 8K+ RPS and 15M+ user activities handled.

Together the two platforms served 50K+ daily active users.

**Gas stations.** I architected and built **ugaz.uz** end to end, the national registry of gas filling stations, and **Cloud U-Gas**, a multi-tenant SaaS for station operators. More than 2,000 stations across Uzbekistan are registered and licensed through it. It handles data, writes and real-time statistics from every station and each of its fuel dispensers at once, and checks vehicles against third-party registries (licensing and more) by recognising number plates from integrated station cameras.

Now Senior Backend Engineer at Leading Business, building Exodim (HR) and LeadingBI (BI + AI).
